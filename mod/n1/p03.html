Books I Actually Have Patience to Read

Sometimes I feel the need to hold a physical book when I am learning something new. Even when a book might be considered old or outdated—or when there is already a GitHub repository, documentation site, or interactive website teaching the exact same subject—I still love owning the books, making inscriptions and notes in the margins, and working through the ideas myself.

Maybe one day I will have my own bookshelf filled with all of the computer science and mathematics books I have worked through, looking back at everything I have studied along the way. Do not give up on books just because there is a repository or a website out there.
