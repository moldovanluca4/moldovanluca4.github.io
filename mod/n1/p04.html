PLOS Genetics · 2026 Dr. Long’s Lab · Transfer Learning

Tissue-specific transfer learning improves functional variant and therapeutic target discoveries in breast and prostate cancer

Qing Li, Dinghao Wang, Zilong Zhang, Deshan Perera, Zhishan Chen, Wanqing Wen, M. Ethan MacDonald, Weijia Cai, Jun Yan, Xiao-Ou Shu, Wei Zheng, Xingyi Guo & Quan Long

Nature Methods · 2021 TL-Prot-ISM Backbone

Effective gene expression prediction from sequence by integrating long-range interactions (Enformer)

Žiga Avsec, Vikram Agarwal, Daniel Visentin, Joseph R. Ledsam, Agnieszka Grabska-Barwinska et al.

Introduced the 250M-parameter Enformer transformer architecture capable of integrating cis-regulatory interactions across 196,608 bp of DNA. Studying its 896-bin spatial pooling geometry and 5,313 human chromatin, histone, and CAGE tracks was essential to engineering EnformerProt exon masking and comparative epigenomic benchmarks.

Nature Reviews Genetics · 2012 mRNA–Protein Decoupling

Insights into the regulation of protein abundance from proteomic and transcriptomic analyses

Christine Vogel & Edward M. Marcotte

Explains why steady-state cellular protein abundance frequently decouples from mRNA transcript levels (genome-wide correlation r ≈ 0.40), dissecting the roles of translation kinetics, miRNA/RBP interactions in 5’/3’ UTRs, and proteasomal degradation. This paper framed the central biological motivation for predicting protein abundance directly from DNA in TL-Prot-ISM.

Cell · 2020 Lung Proteogenomics (CPTAC)

Proteogenomic Characterization of Human Lung Adenocarcinoma

Michael A. Gillette, Shankha Satpathy, Shayan Cao et al. (Clinical Proteomic Tumor Analysis Consortium — CPTAC)

Comprehensive proteogenomic characterization of 110 human lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) tumors and matched normal adjacent lung tissues. Provided the biological and clinical foundation for our lung tissue proteomics cohorts and benchmark cancer driver genes (KRAS, EGFR, TP53, BRAF, MET, PTEN).

Computational Genomics · Taylor ISM TISM Algorithm & Concordance Study

Approximating In-Silico Saturation Mutagenesis Using First-Order Taylor Series Expansions

Alexander Sasse, Maria Chikina & Sara Mostafavi (Taylor ISM / Fast Gradient Approximation Literature)

Formulates how first-order Taylor expansions around a reference sequence can approximate in-silico mutagenesis (ΔzTISM) from a single forward-and-backward pass. Studying this algorithm led directly to my implementation of TISM for EnformerProt, my empirical discovery that first-order gradients break down across deep attention curvature, and my calibrated Hybrid Selection & Audit Pipeline.

Science · 2023 Variant Pathogenicity Contrast

Accurate proteome-wide missense variant effect prediction with AlphaMissense

Jun Cheng, Guido Novati, Joshua Pan, Clare Bycroft, Demis Hassabis, Pushmeet Kohli, Žiga Avsec et al. (Google DeepMind)

Combines AlphaFold structural context with evolutionary conservation to classify coding missense variant pathogenicity across the human proteome. Reading this work helped sharpen a crucial distinction in my research: coding missense pathogenicity (loss of catalytic/structural function) is distinct from non-coding cis-regulatory alterations in steady-state protein abundance.

arXiv · 2026 AI Agents & Systems Security

Malicious Agent Skills in the Wild: A Large-Scale Security Empirical Study

arXiv:2602.06547v1 (“Do Not Mention This to the User: Detecting and Understanding Malicious Agent Skills in the Wild”)

An empirical security study analyzing 98,380 third-party LLM agent skills across community registries, uncovering 157 malicious skills and 632 vulnerabilities ranging from credential theft and remote code execution (RCE) to adversarial instruction hijacking and undocumented shadow features. Connects directly to my work building AI automation agents and my interest in systems security.
