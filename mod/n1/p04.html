Science · 2023 Coding vs. Regulatory Impact

Accurate proteome-wide missense variant effect prediction with AlphaMissense

Jun Cheng, Guido Novati, Joshua Pan, Clare Bycroft, Demis Hassabis, Pushmeet Kohli, Žiga Avsec et al. (Google DeepMind)

Key Takeaway: Predicts coding missense pathogenicity using AlphaFold structural context
